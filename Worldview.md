# Worldview of NomadArchitecture

NomadArchitecture is a cultural operating system expressed through software
architecture. It encodes human intent, continuity and meaning into the shape
of a codebase, so systems stay alive and legible over time, even with AI in
the loop. It is not a blueprint. It is a rhythm of becoming, tested in
practice and revised when practice contradicts it.

First written in November 2025. Revised in October 2026 against nine months of
practice in NomadGameEngineV2. The lineage is in the Worldview Trails beside
this file.

---

## Principles

- **Humans at the center.** AI may scaffold, draft and propose. Humans
  improvise, decide and leave scars. What binds is a human decision
  ([authority](__Docs__/Evidence-And-Authority.md#authority)).
- **Rotation of roles.** The system must outlive any single contributor.
  Continuity comes from trails, structure and shared language, not from
  gatekeepers.
- **No dumping grounds.** Every artifact lives with the owner whose meaning
  would disappear without it. Placement is narrative
  ([placement](__Docs__/Architecture.md#placement)).
- **Docs as parallel architecture.** Documentation is a pillar, structured
  like the code and honest about what each document is: canon, mirage, trail
  or chronicle ([document kinds](__Docs__/README.md#document-kinds)).
- **Trails as proof of life.** Every Feature tells why it exists, how it works
  and the proof that it works. Every document drafted with AI keeps its
  conversation in a Trail.
- **Evidence precedes generalization.** No problem that has not been written
  is solved in advance, and nothing is shared before real use shows that it
  is shared ([promotion](__Docs__/Architecture.md#promotion)).
- **Say what you know, at the strength you know it.** AI fabricates when truth
  is absent; the remedy is evidence, not trust. Observation, interpretation
  and the unknown stay distinct
  ([claims](__Docs__/Evidence-And-Authority.md#claims)).
- **Show, then tell.** Canon speaks for itself through the repository: a tree
  that explains itself, an [example](examples/Greeting/README.md) that follows
  the rules and proves them, and a [check](Scripts/README.md) that fails when
  the documents lie.

## Canonical Skeleton

| Folder | Essence | Promise |
|---|---|---|
| **Hosts/** | WHY: purpose and intent | Replaceable surfaces, never the soul |
| **Features/** | WHAT: capabilities | Sovereign promises, each carrying its story |
| **Foundations/** | HOW: mechanisms | Hardened enablers that never dominate |

| **__Docs__/** | Memory: the story trailhead | Canon, trails, mirages and chronicles |

The rules for each pillar are in [Architecture](__Docs__/Architecture.md#pillars).

## Emergence Rhythm

Systems tend to grow in a sequence:

1. **Host sparks.** Intent and code begin together in one runnable place.
2. **A seam cries out.** Real pressure shows what is missing.
3. **Foundations harden.** Reusable mechanism leaves the Host.
4. **Features earn their meaning.** Shared meaning becomes a Feature only after
   real use proves the need.
5. **Core remembers.** Language crystallises into Core only when it is common
   across pillars.

This is not a prescription but a pattern observed in practice: scars first,
stories second, stability last.

---

## The Shaman

The Shaman is not an architect. The Shaman keeps the fire alive, records
scars, interprets meaning and transmits continuity. The role is fleeting,
rotating, and never owns the system. When something must become binding, the
Shaman is the human who decides.

---

## Closing Thought

- Host is the tent where the compass is kept.
- Features are the clans that sprout.
- `_Connectors` are the ligaments between owners.
- Foundations are the ground that hardens beneath.
- Core is the memory that binds.
- The Shaman ensures continuity, not by owning, but by interpreting and
  transmitting.

The result is a living commons: clear enough to teach, strong enough to
endure, and humble enough to change.
