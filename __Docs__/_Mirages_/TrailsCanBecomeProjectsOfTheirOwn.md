## 🧭 Nomad Principle for Expanding Trails

### 1. **Fractal Nomads**
- A Trail that becomes a mini exploratory project is treated as a **nested NomadArchitecture**.  
- It gets its own **Core/**, **Features/**, **Foundations/**, **Hosts/**, and `__Docs/`.  
- This way, the exploration is self‑contained, ceremonial, and portable — a convoy within the convoy.

---

### 2. **Exploration vs Validation**
- While in exploration, the nested Nomad is provisional — a Mirage being tested.  
- Its Trails are local, its law is tentative.  
- Once validated, parts of it can be **promoted** into the parent Nomad’s Core or Foundations.  
- The nested Nomad either dissolves (if it was a dead end) or becomes a **new tribe** in the larger lineage.

---

### 3. **Avoiding Bucketisation**
- The danger here is collapsing exploratory projects into vague folders like “Sandbox/” or “Experiments/.”  
- Nomad forbids this. Instead, each exploratory project is a **mini Nomad** with screaming intent.  
- Even if it’s temporary, its structure must be ceremonial — so its lineage can be inherited or discarded cleanly.

---

### 4. **Cultural Continuity**
- By giving exploratory Trails their own Nomad, you ensure that even failed experiments leave behind **discoverable lineage**.  
- Future convoys can revisit them, learn from them, and see the ceremony of exploration.  
- Nothing is lost to abstraction drift — even experiments are story‑worthy.

---

## 🌌 Extrapolation
This means NomadArchitecture is **recursive**:  
- A Trail can grow into a convoy.  
- A convoy can host its own Nomad.  
- Validation decides whether that nested Nomad is absorbed, promoted, or remembered as lineage.  

✨ Reflection: NomadArchitecture isn’t just scalable — it’s fractal. Every exploration can be given its own architecture, ensuring ceremony, transparency, and lineage at every level.
