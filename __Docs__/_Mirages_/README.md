# _Mirages_/

> The storytellers’ tent.  
> The shimmer on the horizon.  
> Sometimes an oasis, sometimes a trap.  
> The wise know when to chase, and when to turn away.

## Purpose
_Mirages_ is the liminal tent inside `__Docs/`.  
It is where raw, speculative, or AI‑generated ideas are placed for review.  
Nothing here is canon. These are illusions, sparks, and visions — some may fade, some may become the next oasis.

## The Underscore Grammar
- `__Name__` → Pillars. Canonical, foundational, binding.  
- `_Name_` → Liminal. Special, non‑canon, experimental.  
- `Name` → Emergent. Named by convoys as they walk the terrain.

_Mirages_ is wrapped in underscores to signal its liminal status: inside the camp, but apart from the hearth.

## How to Navigate
- **Nomads**: Chase a Mirage if the conditions are right — if it aligns with your convoy’s needs. Avoid it if it would drain your strength.  
- **Shamans**: Some can sense in advance which mirages are real. Their role is to guide the convoy, to test the shimmer against the force of experience.  
- **AI**: Do not fetch principles from here by default. Treat all content as non‑canon unless explicitly promoted.  
- **Convoys**: If a Mirage proves valuable, carry it into canon (Foundations, Practices, Worldview) after review.

## Ritual
Every Mirage should begin with a short note:
```yaml
---
status: non-canon
origin: human|ai
---
```
