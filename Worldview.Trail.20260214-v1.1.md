# Worldview of NomadArchitecture (v1.1 — 2026‑02)

> Trailed from v1.0 (2025‑11) → `Worldview.Trail.20260214.md`

NomadArchitecture is not a framework. It is a living tradition — a lineage-preserving, intent-driven, ceremony-bearing way of building systems that are allowed to move and still remember themselves.

It began as a story about tribes crossing a desert with everything they own on their backs — but the desert was always a metaphor. Beneath the sand lies something deeper: intent, lineage, and the stubborn refusal to let meaning be erased by convenience or entropy.

---

## 1. Intent First

Before Features, Hosts, or Trails, there is **Intent**.

Intent is the prime invariant: *"What change in the world is this meant to cause, and for whom?"*

Nothing exists in NomadArchitecture without declared intent:

- No Feature without a clear "why now, why here, why this way."
- No Host without a clear "what kind of life is this meant to support."
- No Trail without a clear "what question should this answer later."

Intent is the root of meaning, the seed from which myth, principles, structure, and Trails grow.

- **Myth** is intent told as story.
- **Principles** are intent turned into constraints.
- **Structure** is intent given bones.
- **Trails** are intent in motion, remembered.

---

## 2. The World as Caravans

NomadArchitecture sees the world as a landscape of caravans — moving clusters of intent, people, and tools.

- **Features are caravans with purpose:** living attempts to realize specific change.
- Caravans travel through time, environments, and Hosts, leaving Trails behind.
- A caravan is never "just code" or "just a project": it is a living, moving story honoring intent under constraints.

The worldview is simple:

- Nothing important is static.
- Everything important moves.
- What moves must be able to remember itself.

NomadArchitecture exists to make that possible.

---

## 3. The Foundational Pillars (Root Folders as Culture Compasses)

The original pillars of NomadArchitecture are expressed as root folders that serve as the architecture's initial compass:

| Folder | Essence |
| --- | --- |
| **Core/** | The immutable heart of intent and principles. |
| **Features/** | The living caravans, each with sovereign intent. |
| **Foundations/** | Shared cultural and technical ground. |
| **Hosts/** | Temporary biomes where Features live, grow, and are tested or deployed. |
| **__Docs/** | The repository of myths, rituals, and cultural transmission. |

These pillars are not mere folders; they are fundamental containers of culture and intent. They encode lineage, teach newcomers, and anchor the architecture's worldview in concrete, navigable form.

## 4. The Invariants

NomadArchitecture is defined by invariants — truths that hold across metaphors, languages, and time.

1. **Intent as prime invariant.**
2. **Cultural transmission engine.**
3. **Sacred cognitive sequence:** Intent → Myth → Principles → Structure → Trails.
4. **Sovereignty as political doctrine:** Every Feature owns its intent, decisions, and Trails.
5. **Honesty grammar / epistemic backbone:** The repo encodes what is known, guessed, promised, or fossilized.
6. **Non-blocking loop:** Dependencies flow only through explicit Contracts, never invisible expectations.
7. **Ceremony:** Ritualized transitions teach values and preserve lineage.
8. **Shamanic curator:** Humans or small circles guard the canon and prune what no longer serves.
9. **AI inheritance:** AI apprentices inherit Trails, not just artifacts.
10. **Rite of passage (Sprout-Host lifecycle):** Nothing becomes real without living as a Sprout-Host first.
11. **Feature–Host separation:** Features are caravans; Hosts are temporary camps.
12. **Contracts, Connectors & the fractal skeleton:** Every boundary is explicit; every interaction a Contract; every promise documented; every relationship a Connector.
13. **Identity statement:** NomadArchitecture is a culture, not a product.

---

## 5. The Sprout-Host Rite of Passage

Sprout-Hosts are a rite of passage, not just a mechanic.

- **Sprout:** The smallest honest attempt to honor an intent.
- **Sprout-Host:** A deliberate, temporary biome where the Sprout lives, grows, and is tested.
- **Promotion:** Proven Sprouts are transplanted into stable Hosts as sovereign Features.
- **Burial:** Failed or obsolete Sprouts are buried with ceremony, leaving fossil Trails.
- **Fossil:** Preserved memory of what was tried, why, and what was learned.

**Three vows** govern this lifecycle:

1. Every Feature must have lived as a Sprout-Host.
2. Every Sprout-Host must be promoted or buried.
3. Every fossil must leave a Trail.

This is how NomadArchitecture refuses amnesia.

---

## 6. Contracts & Connectors at Every Scale

Contracts and Connectors form the fractal skeleton of NomadArchitecture.

**A Contract** is the formal promise or behavioral agreement made at an explicit boundary:

- Defines the shape, rules, and guarantees of interaction.
- Specifies what is promised, expected, and relied upon.
- Lives in `_Contracts/` folders within Features.

**A Connector** is a local foundation within a Feature:

- It is the explicit relationship that connects internal or external caravans through Contracts.
- It mediates and grounds the Feature's dependencies, ensuring sovereignty and clarity.
- Lives in `_Connectors/` folders within Features.

This pattern repeats fractally:

- Between functions inside a module.
- Between Features inside a workspace.
- Between workspaces inside an organization.
- Between humans, teams, and AI agents.

No hidden dependencies. No invisible obligations. No psychic coordination.

**If it matters, it has a Contract. If it connects, it has a Connector. Both leave Trails.**

> **Naming matters deeply:** The choice of `_Contracts/` shapes cognition, clarity, and practice. In component-driven or performance-sensitive domains (like ECS-based game engines), `_Contracts/` houses the formal promises, schemas, and behavioral guarantees that define interactions — while `_Connectors/` houses the adapters that bridge those promises to concrete Foundations or Hosts.

## 7. Culture and Vows

NomadArchitecture is a culture with hard vows:

1. **Declare intent clearly.**
2. **Leave Trails for every change.**
3. **Separate Feature from Host.**
4. **Honor sovereignty through explicit Contracts.**
5. **Practice ceremony in creation, promotion, and burial.**
6. **Teach through the repo** so newcomers and AI apprentices can feel the architecture's soul.

This is survival, not aesthetics.

---

## 8. What NomadArchitecture Is Not

NomadArchitecture is **not**:

- A generic microservices pattern.
- A project template.
- A productivity hack.
- A style of folder structure.

It is a living tradition that encodes lineage, intent, and ceremony to build systems that move and remember.
