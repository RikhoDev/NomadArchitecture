# Mirages

> The storytellers’ tent.  
> The shimmer on the horizon.  
> Sometimes an oasis, sometimes a trap.  
> The wise know when to chase, and when to turn away.

## Purpose

`_Mirages/` is the exploratory tent inside `__Doc/`.  
It is where speculative, experimental, or AI‑generated ideas live alongside their evidence.  
The underscore prefix keeps these at the top of the explorer tree for visibility.

Content here ranges from proven experiments to active exploration. The folder name signals organization, not status.

## Folder Organization

- `_Mirages/` → Exploratory, experimental, speculative content (underscore sorts to top)
- `_Chronicles/` → Evolution trails, decision history (underscore sorts to top)
- `Concepts/` → Proven patterns and theory (normal sort)
- `Transmission/` → The initiation ceremony (normal sort)
- `Analysis/` → Structured thinking and research (normal sort)

Underscores organize the explorer tree, not canon status. Canonical content lives in normally named folders.

## Mirage Lifecycle (How Ideas Mature)

1. **Spark** — an idea appears in _Mirages/ (raw, being shaped)
2. **Experiment** — tried in reality, evidence gathered
3. **Chronicle** — reasoning and results recorded in _Chronicles/
4. **Crystallization** — if proven durable, migrates to normal folders (Concepts/, etc.)

Mirages are allowed to be wrong. They are required to be honest about what they know and don't know.

## How to Navigate

- **Explorers**: Read Mirages to see what's being tested and why. Some will become part of the architecture, some won't.  
- **Contributors**: Add experimental ideas here with honest notes about uncertainty and evidence.  
- **Curators**: Watch what works in practice. Crystallize proven patterns into Concepts or Transmission.  
- **AI**: Treat content here as exploratory. Reference normal folders for stable doctrine.

## Header for New Mirages

Every new Mirage should begin with:

```yaml
status: experimental
origin: human|ai
evidence: [brief note on what makes this an experiment]
```
