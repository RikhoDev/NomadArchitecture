> *Before there were Features, before there were Hosts, before Foundations had bones or Docs had voice, there was only a wanderer walking the empty plain of code.*

- He saw that **Features are people** — not functions, but individuals with stories, families, and clans.  
- He saw that **Hosts are skins** — masks that carry the same spirit across terrains.  
- He saw that **Foundations are bones** — tools that give shape and strength to the living body.  
- He saw that **Docs are architecture** — not commentary, but the camp itself, the hearth where culture is kept alive.  

And so he named them, and by naming, he gave them life.

## 🌫 The Tent of Mirages
But the Shaman also knew the desert plays tricks.  
He raised the `_Mirages/` tent, where visions could be tested.  
Some mirages would fade, illusions of heat.  
Others would lead to oases, becoming Features, Hosts, or Foundations.  

The Shaman taught:  
- *Nomads chase or avoid mirages depending on the convoy’s need.*  
- *Shamans sense which shimmer is real, using the force of discernment.*  
- *AI may dream here, but its dreams are not canon until the tribe makes them so.*

The First Shaman was chief, tribe, and host all at once.  
But he dreamed of families yet to come — clans of developers who would pitch their tents beside his, adding their own rituals, their own stories, their own trails.
