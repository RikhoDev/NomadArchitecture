# Canon change: mobility returns to the Pillars

Decided by the assistant under the delegation "You now got to mix it all and
see what goes. [...] be nomad." Marked `pillars` in Architecture's
`decided_by_assistant`, until a canon owner reads it.

## What changed

- **Pillars, Feature:** one sentence. A Feature can pack up and leave: outside
  its `_Connectors` it needs only Core, so it can join another Host or another
  system with its contracts, its proof and its Trail.
- **Pillars and Promotion, sources:** SampleGame is now cited where each rule
  first appeared.
  - Family mobility is in its Copilot instructions of 2025-08-03.
  - The two-consumer promotion rule is its vocabulary-promotion decision
    record of the same day.

  The rule text of Promotion is unchanged.

Replaces nothing. It restores a promise that `git log -S` finds nowhere in
this repository's history.

## Why

Reading SampleGame's history showed that mobility was the method's first
promise. On 2025-08-03, families that "pack up and join new convoys with zero
friction" were the reason for the pillars. The promise never reached this
repository: its canon held the shape that makes mobility possible, but not the
purpose.

To an organisation, a capability that can move between teams and products is
the most valuable claim. It was also the least evidenced.

## How it is held

Billing's rule "a Feature reaches another only through its connectors" became
`Every_Feature_can_pack_up_and_leave`. The new test covers the old one's case,
and also rejects Foundations and Hosts. Keeping both would have duplicated a
rule, so the count stays at seven.

A mutant that reaches Invoicing from Reminders outside the connector fails
the new rule.

## What is not shown

No Feature has been moved into another system. The test holds the dependency
shape that makes a move possible. A real move, such as Reminders joining a
second system, is the evidence still owed.
