# Layer 03 — The Five Pillars (The Civilization That Holds Everything)

After years of watching codebases collapse under their own weight, I kept seeing the same pattern:

**Systems fail when the boundaries between concerns blur.**

So NomadArchitecture has five pillars — not arbitrary, but **discovered**.

Think of them not as folders, but as **roles in a civilization**:

| Pillar | Anthropological Role | Why It Exists |
| ------ | -------------------- | ------------- |
| **Core** | **Lingua Franca** | The shared language that lets sovereign clans communicate without becoming entangled |
| **Features** | **Clans** | Autonomous tribes with their own customs, tools, and sovereignty — but speaking the common tongue |
| **Foundations** | **Chosen Ground** | The hardened paths (roads, aqueducts, bridges) that emerged from repeated need, not imposed planning |
| **Hosts** | **Skins** | Replaceable outer shells that let the civilization meet different worlds without changing its soul |
| **Docs** | **Cultural Layer** | The myths, chronicles, and transmission mechanisms that keep knowledge alive across generations |

This isn't a hierarchy. It's an **ecosystem**:

**Core speaks the language everyone understands.**  
**Features are clans that explore independently.**  
**Foundations are the roads that crystallize from repeated paths.**  
**Hosts are skins that adapt to environments.**  
**Docs are the oral tradition that preserves it all.**

Every decision you make will map to one of these five pillars.  
"Should this be in Core or Features?" becomes answerable.

**When you'll need it:**  
The moment you're designing a new capability and wondering where it belongs — the Five Pillars give you the answer.
