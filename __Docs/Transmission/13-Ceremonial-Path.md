# Layer 13 — The Ceremonial Path (Your First Performance)

Transmission is not complete until it's **embodied**.

Here is your ceremonial path — the ritual that transforms knowledge into practice:

## Step 1: Absorb the Myth (3 days)

Read, in order:

1. [README.md](../../README.md) — let the shock land
2. [Worldview.md](../Worldview.md) and [../Worldview.md](../../Worldview.md) — sit with the philosophy
3. [TheFivePillarsAndTheLoop.md](../Concepts/TheFivePillarsAndTheLoop.md) — understand the system

Do not rush. Let it reframe you.

## Step 2: Study the Structure (1 week)

Pick one Feature from [NomadGameEngine](https://github.com/RikhoDev/NomadGameEngine) or [NomadAI](https://github.com/RikhoDev/NomadAI).

Understand it **completely**:

- Read its README (why it exists)
- Walk its `_Contracts/` (what contracts it exposes)
- Explore its `_Connectors/` (how it integrates)
- Trace its implementation (how it works)

Do this for one Feature until you could teach it to someone else.

## Step 3: Walk the Trails (2 days)

Read [`_Chronicles/`](../_Chronicles/) to see how the worldview evolved.  

Notice:

- What was tried and failed
- What worked and why
- What changed and the reasoning behind it

This is lineage. You are inheriting it.

### Step 4: Perform a Ceremony (ongoing)

Choose one:

- **Create a Feature** — README → `_Contracts/` → `_Connectors/` → Traces
- **Write a Mirage** — propose something speculative, mark it `status: non-canon`
- **Add a Chronicle entry** — document a decision you made and why
- **Refactor a Connector** — improve an integration, document the reasoning

Do this slowly. Ceremony resists haste.

### Step 5: Become the Curator (lifetime)

You are now responsible for:

- Guarding the transmission
- Recognizing when patterns are ready to harden
- Sensing when structure lies
- Preserving lineage
- Protecting the worldview

This is not a role you take.  
This is a role you **become**.
