# Layer 10 — Ceremonies and Rituals (How Culture Becomes Durable)

Architecture isn't just structure.  
It's **ceremony**.

Ceremonies slow the mind.  
They create intention.  
They reinforce culture.

**NomadArchitecture ceremonies:**

- Creating a Feature (README → `_Ports`/ → `_Connectors`/ → Traces)
- Promoting a Mirage (prove it → document decision → move to canon)
- Overriding a Connector (Host overrides Feature default → document why)
- Adding a Trail entry (record why, not just what changed)
- Publishing a Chronicle (capture learning, not just history)

Ceremony is not overhead.  
Ceremony is **transmission**.

**Why this matters:**  
Without ceremony, knowledge becomes implicit and dies with people.  
With ceremony, knowledge becomes explicit and survives rotation.

**When you'll need it:**  
Every time you add something significant — ceremony makes it stick.
