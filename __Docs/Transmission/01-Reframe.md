# Layer 01 — The Reframe (Start Here — This Changes Everything)

Most architecture fails before it fails technically.  
It fails because **people lose orientation**.

Teams stop knowing:

- Why we're building this
- What's allowed to change
- Where a new idea belongs
- How to keep the core simple while complexity grows

NomadArchitecture starts with a single cognitive rupture:

> **What if software could transmit intent, not just function?**

This is not a technical question.  
It's a **cultural** question.

If your codebase could teach itself to newcomers...  
If your structure could explain your decisions...  
If your folders could communicate your worldview...

Then architecture becomes **self-transmitting**.

**Read first:**

- [README.md](../../README.md) — the mythic doorway (the shock that opens the mind)
- [Worldview.md](../Worldview.md) and [../Worldview.md](../../Worldview.md) — the philosophy that everything else builds on

**Why this layer matters:**  
Without this reframe, everything that follows will feel like "just another methodology."  
With this reframe, you'll see why structure choices are cultural choices, why naming is promise-making, why trails matter as much as code.

**When you'll need it:**  
The moment someone asks "why not just use folders normally?" — you'll know the answer isn't technical.
